# Chess Game Instructions

Welcome to the Chess Game created by Abenezer Markos!

## How to Play

1. The game starts with White's turn.
2. Click on a piece to select it. The selected piece will be highlighted.
3. Click on a valid destination square to move the piece.
4. The game will automatically switch turns after a valid move.
5. The game status will update to show whose turn it is, if a player is in check, or if checkmate has occurred.

## Special Moves

- Castling: Move your king two squares towards a rook, and the rook will automatically move to the other side of the king.
- En Passant: If a pawn moves two squares forward and lands beside an enemy pawn, the enemy pawn can capture it as if it had only moved one square.
- Pawn Promotion: When a pawn reaches the opposite end of the board, it can be promoted to any other piece (usually a queen).

## Game History

- The move history is displayed on the right side of the board.
- Click on any move in the history to review the board state at that moment.
- Use the "Start" and "Latest" buttons to quickly navigate to the beginning or end of the game.

## Saving and Loading Games

- Games are automatically saved after each move.
- To start a new game, click the "Start New Game" button in the Saved Games section.
- To load a previously saved game, click on it in the Saved Games list.

Enjoy playing chess!
